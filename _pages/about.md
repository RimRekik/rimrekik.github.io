I am a **postdoctoral researcher in machine learning** in the [Imagine team](https://liris.cnrs.fr/equipe/imagine), LIRIS, École Centrale de Lyon. I develop **deep-learning methods to model and analyse complex data** (molecular graphs and mass-spectrometry signals, 3D shapes and human motion, images) across **biomedical, computer vision and computer graphics** applications. I evaluate them with **rigorous protocols that are quantitative, qualitative and perceptual**.

### Research

**Biomedical data: proteomics and clinical microbiology** *(current work)*.
I design hierarchical graph neural networks that represent peptides at the molecular level to predict their mass-spectrometry signals, including for chemically modified peptides. I also apply computer-vision models to mass-spectrometry data for bacterial identification.

**3D humans: 4D vision, animation and perception** *(PhD, Inria Morpheo/Kinetix and VirtUs)*.
I developed a correspondence-free, online method that retargets motion between 3D humans of any shape, from raw 4D captures ([3DV 2024](/publications/2024-Human-Motion-Retargeting/)). I contributed to the 4DHumanOutfit capture dataset ([CVIU 2023](/publications/2023-4DHumanOutfit/)). On perception, I wrote a survey on the realism of virtual human animation ([Eurographics STAR 2024](/publications/2024-StarVH/)) and built a perceptual dataset and learned metrics that predict how people judge animation quality ([IEEE TVCG 2025](/publications/2025-QualityAssessment-TVCG/)).

**Images: inverse problems.**
I work on learned, spatially adaptive regularisation for blind image denoising ([EUSIPCO 2025](/publications/2025-BlindDenoising-EUSIPCO/)).

**How I evaluate.** I evaluate with quantitative metrics, qualitative analysis and user studies that measure human perception, using protocols designed to avoid data leakage and to reflect what matters for the application.
