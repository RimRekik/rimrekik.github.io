I am a **postdoctoral researcher in machine learning** in the [Imagine team](https://liris.cnrs.fr/equipe/imagine), LIRIS, École Centrale de Lyon.

I build deep-learning solutions **end to end, from data analysis and model design to rigorous evaluation**, and I adapt them to new application domains. My work covers **3D human motion and 4D vision**, **computer graphics and perception**, **image restoration** and, today, **biomedical mass spectrometry**.

### Highlights
- **Publications in leading venues**: IEEE TVCG, Computer Graphics Forum (Eurographics State-of-the-Art Report), Computer Vision and Image Understanding (all Q1 journals), International Conference on 3D Vision (3DV, joint first author) and EUSIPCO. See [Publications](/publications/).
- **Open science**: released code ([motion retargeting](https://gitlab.inria.fr/rrekikdi/human-motion-retargeting2023), [4DHumanQA](https://gitlab.inria.fr/rrekikdi/4dhumanqa)) and datasets ([4DHumanPercept](https://gitlab.inria.fr/rrekikdi/4dhumanqa), [4DHumanOutfit](https://kinovis.inria.fr/4dhumanoutfit/)).
- **Adaptability**: from 3D virtual humans in my PhD to proteomics and clinical microbiology in my postdoc, with a new graph neural network model for peptide spectra already [in preparation](/publications/2026-PepH-GAT/).
- **Rigorous evaluation**: quantitative metrics and leakage-free protocols, qualitative analysis, and perceptual user studies (study design, ethics, statistical analysis).
- **Teaching and supervision**: about 145 hours of teaching from first-year undergraduate to engineering-school level; co-supervision of a master's student.

### Research
**Biomedical data** *(postdoc, current)*. Hierarchical graph neural networks that model peptides at the molecular level to predict their mass-spectrometry spectra, and computer-vision models for bacterial identification from mass-spectrometry data.

**3D humans: 4D vision, animation and perception** *(PhD, Inria Morpheo/Kinetix and VirtUs)*. Correspondence-free online motion retargeting from raw 4D captures ([3DV 2024](/publications/2024-Human-Motion-Retargeting/)), the 4DHumanOutfit dataset ([CVIU 2023](/publications/2023-4DHumanOutfit/)), a survey on realistic virtual humans ([Eurographics STAR 2024](/publications/2024-StarVH/)) and learned perceptual quality metrics for 3D animation ([IEEE TVCG 2025](/publications/2025-QualityAssessment-TVCG/)).

**Images** *(master's research)*. Learned, spatially adaptive regularisation for blind image denoising ([EUSIPCO 2025](/publications/2025-BlindDenoising-EUSIPCO/)).

More details on the [Research](/research/) page.
