## Summary
"Realism" is used everywhere in virtual human research, but it covers many different notions. This survey clarifies **how multiple factors shape the perceived realism of animated virtual humans**. We review the perceptual studies that assess realism, organize the **features manipulated to increase it** (appearance, motion and their interplay), and review the **evaluation methods**, both subjective (user studies) and objective (metrics), developed to measure it. We conclude with open problems and research directions, in particular evaluation measures that agree with human perception.

This survey motivated my later work on perceptual quality metrics for 3D human animation ([IEEE TVCG 2025](/publications/2025-QualityAssessment-TVCG/)).
