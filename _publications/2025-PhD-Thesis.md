*Original French title: Génération et évaluation des mouvements humains en 3D.*
Inria Morpheo (Grenoble) and VirtUs (Rennes) teams · Supervisors: Dr. Stefanie Wuhrer and Dr. Anne-Hélène Olivier.

## Summary
Animated 3D virtual humans are central to computer graphics, virtual and augmented reality, and digital content creation. This thesis addresses two complementary questions: **how to generate** 3D human motion that stays faithful to both the motion and the body, and **how to evaluate** whether the result looks right to people.

- **Generation.** A correspondence-free, online method retargets motion between arbitrary 3D bodies. It combines learned skeleton extraction, kinematic-tree-aware motion transfer and a learned deformation model ([3DV 2024](/publications/2024-Human-Motion-Retargeting/)).
- **Understanding realism.** A survey defines what "realistic" means for virtual human animation, which features drive it and how it is measured ([Eurographics STAR 2024](/publications/2024-StarVH/)).
- **Evaluation.** The 4DHumanPercept dataset, built from real 4D captures ([4DHumanOutfit, CVIU 2023](/publications/2023-4DHumanOutfit/)) with controlled distortions rated in a user study, and a learned quality metric reaching 90% correlation with human judgements ([IEEE TVCG 2025](/publications/2025-QualityAssessment-TVCG/)).
